# Compliance Checklist

## Museums and Historical Monuments

- Paid commentary inside Musée de France, monuments historiques and regulated cultural spaces must be provided only by verified `guide-conférencier` holders.
- Admin must record qualification review status.
- Public pages may show "certified guide" badges but must not expose full card number or document image.
- Ordinary local assistants may provide life assistance, translation assistance, restaurant communication, shopping assistance, public transport help, family-friendly support and pre-boarding support.
- Ordinary local assistants must not be described as professional museum guides unless verified.

## Transport

- Ordinary providers must not use private vehicles for paid passenger transport.
- Paid transport must be fulfilled only by legal VTC, taxi or transport professionnel providers.
- MVP displays request intent only.
- No real-time ride hailing.
- No fare collection.
- Admin records qualification review state.

## Seine Cruises

- MVP does not collect ticket fees.
- MVP does not generate tickets or QR codes.
- MVP does not promise refunds, discounts, inventory or official partnership.
- MVP may collect request intent and show "partner official link coming soon".

## Travel Packages

The MVP must not sell flights, hotels, accommodation, flight-hotel bundles, hotel-cruise bundles, accommodation-transfer bundles, multi-day travel packages or automatic packaged products.

## Emergency Assistance

- The platform does not replace police, medical institutions, fire services, consulates, lawyers or official emergency channels.
- No medical diagnosis.
- No legal advice.
- No promise of 24-hour human response.
- Users in immediate danger must be directed to official emergency numbers.

## Travel Mart And Physical Products

- MVP does not show real inventory.
- MVP does not create real product checkout, payment or order confirmation.
- MVP does not create real delivery times.
- Direct physical product sale requires CGV, price display review, delivery terms, refund rules and withdrawal rights review.
- Food products require labels, allergen, storage and merchant source review before launch.
- Alcohol, medicine, tobacco, e-cigarettes, nicotine products and high-risk regulated products are not sold.
- Merchant contracts and image/logo authorization are required before public partner claims.

## Rentals And Hotel Delivery

- MVP rental pages are request-only.
- No real deposits, rents, damage charges or late-return fees are collected.
- Cleaning, damage, late-return and return policies require legal and merchant review.
- Hotel front desk delivery is a placeholder until hotel policy and merchant fulfillment rules are confirmed.
- Do not collect or display hotel room numbers.
- Users must be told: acceptance and delivery point depend on hotel policy.
