# Hotel Delivery SOP

Hotel front desk delivery is a placeholder until partner, legal and hotel policy confirmation.

Rules:

- Do not collect or display hotel room numbers.
- Ask only hotel name/area when necessary.
- Confirm whether the hotel accepts front desk delivery.
- Confirm allowed delivery point and ID requirements.
- Do not promise delivery time.
- Do not create fake delivery orders.

User-facing copy:

> 是否接受配送以及交付地点，以酒店规定为准。请勿填写房间号。请在酒店允许的情况下使用前台交付。
