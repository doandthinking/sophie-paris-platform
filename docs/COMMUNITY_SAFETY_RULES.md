# Community Safety Rules

- Adults only for travel-buddy posts.
- Meet in public places.
- Tell friends or family where you go.
- Do not enter strangers' private homes.
- Do not reveal hotel room numbers.
- Do not share passport photos, bank cards or passwords.
- Do not transfer money to strangers.
- Do not accept suspicious private paid arrangements.
- Leave immediately if harassed.
- Use report and block features.
- Call official emergency numbers in danger.
- The platform cannot guarantee every user's identity.
- Offline meetings require caution.
