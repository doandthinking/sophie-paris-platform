# Competitor Benchmark

This is qualitative analysis only. It does not claim precise market share or private data.

## Klook

- Learn: clear activity cards, order completion points, strong mobile booking flow.
- Do not copy: generic marketplace breadth before supply quality.
- MVP fit: service cards and request funnel.
- Future fit: partner offers and loyalty.
- Risk: ticketing compliance and partner contracts.

## Airbnb Experiences

- Learn: trust, experience-led storytelling, host profiles.
- Do not copy: implying every local can perform regulated guiding.
- MVP fit: curated experiences.
- Future fit: stronger provider profiles.
- Risk: safety and liability for offline activities.

## GetYourGuide / Viator

- Learn: activity categorization, cancellation clarity, review UX.
- Do not copy: fake inventory or instant confirmation without backend operations.
- MVP fit: service detail pages.
- Future fit: structured availability.
- Risk: ticketing and seller-of-record complexity.

## Trip.com

- Learn: membership and points psychology.
- Do not copy: broad OTA scope with flights and hotels.
- MVP fit: demo points rules.
- Future fit: member benefits after legal review.
- Risk: rewards terms and accounting.

## Couchsurfing / Meetup / Travello

- Learn: destination communities, public events, travel-buddy interest matching.
- Do not copy: open-ended stranger contact without safety controls.
- MVP fit: public activity board and safety rules.
- Future fit: verified accounts and safe messaging.
- Risk: moderation, minors, harassment, privacy.

## Withlocals / ToursByLocals

- Learn: local provider and expert profiles.
- Do not copy: unclear distinction between licensed guiding and casual assistance.
- MVP fit: provider application with badges.
- Future fit: verified provider pages.
- Risk: qualification review and claims.

## Official French Resources

- Learn: emergency flow clarity and source attribution.
- Do not copy: official identity, logos, or authority.
- MVP fit: resource navigation with verification dates.
- Future fit: periodically verified resource database.
- Risk: outdated emergency or consular information.
