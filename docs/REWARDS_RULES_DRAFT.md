# Rewards Rules Draft

Paris Welcome Points are DEMO in MVP.

- No cash value.
- Not withdrawable.
- Not transferable.
- Not redeemable for real tickets.
- Not redeemable for unsigned partner benefits.
- Emergency assistance must not require points.
- Refund, cancellation or fraud orders earn no points.
- Points earned on reversed orders should be reversed.
- Review points must not depend on positive ratings.
- No reward for five-star-only feedback.
