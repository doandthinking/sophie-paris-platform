# Merchant Onboarding Checklist

Collect:

- Merchant name.
- Type.
- Contact person.
- Email, phone and WeChat.
- District.
- Official website.
- Product or service scope.
- Pickup support.
- Hotel front desk delivery support.
- District delivery support.
- Chinese visitor welcome kit interest.
- Official image availability.
- Pilot interest.

Before launch:

- Merchant contract.
- Image authorization.
- Price and stock display rules.
- Fulfillment rules.
- Consumer complaint handling.
- Legal review.
