# Product Spec

## Vision

Paris Local Link / 巴黎安心行 helps international visitors in Paris, especially Chinese-speaking visitors, experience the city with clearer information, safer local support and better compliance boundaries.

One-line positioning:

> 用熟悉的语言，更安心、更深入地体验巴黎。

## MVP Scope

- Paris only.
- Four locales: `zh-CN`, `zh-TW`, `fr`, `en`.
- Local mock data.
- localStorage forms.
- No real payment.
- No real ticketing.
- No real authentication.
- No real chat.
- No real reward redemption.
- No fake official partnerships.
- No fake reviews, orders, ratings or user counts.

## Core Modules

- Paris Signature demo experiences.
- Certified cultural guiding.
- Seine cruise request-only section.
- Local life assistance.
- Emergency resource navigation.
- Paris Welcome Points demo.
- Real review structure with DEMO placeholders.
- Paris Together community safety MVP.
- Provider application.
- Admin demo.
- Partner CRM and partner presentation page.

## Success Criteria

- Visitors understand the service categories and compliance boundaries.
- Potential providers understand qualification requirements.
- Partners can see a low-risk pilot proposal.
- The team can collect early demand without pretending that commercial operations are already live.
