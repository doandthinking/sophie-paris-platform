# Partnership Strategy

## First Contact Positioning

Do not ask for broad exclusivity at first. Propose a low-risk pilot for Chinese-speaking and international visitors in Paris.

## Good First Ask

- Four-language landing page.
- Official image authorization.
- Official redirect link or channel code.
- Group ticket rules.
- Chinese visitor request collection.
- One or two Chinese-language pilot sessions per month.
- Sunset cruise or small cultural group test.
- Family-friendly theme.
- Low-season test.
- Chinese pre-visit information.

## Partner Keeps Control

- Ticketing.
- Timeslots.
- Prices.
- Venue rules.
- Sailing rules.
- Refund conditions.
- Brand.
- Images.
- Final confirmation.

## Platform Provides

- Four-language page.
- Chinese visitor communication.
- Request collection.
- Visitor reminders.
- Initial certified-guide coordination.
- Content localization.
- Pilot demand report.
- Visitor feedback collection.
