# Reviews Policy Draft

## Sources

- `demo`: for interface demonstration only, not included in ratings.
- `verified-platform-booking`: completed platform booking.
- `historical-client-authorized`: previously served customer with explicit consent.
- `partner-authorized-import`: written partner authorization required.

## Principles

- No fake travelers.
- No fake photos.
- No fake orders.
- No fake star ratings.
- No fake review counts.
- Negative but reasonable reviews should not be hidden.
- Reviews may be rejected or redacted for illegal content, privacy disclosure, insults or irrelevant content.
- Any points reward must be disclosed and unrelated to score.
