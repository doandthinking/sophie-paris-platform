# Pre Launch Checklist

## Technical

- Lint passes.
- Build passes.
- TypeScript passes.
- Four-language routes pass.
- Mobile and desktop tested.
- Forms and localStorage tested.
- Admin tested.
- No console errors.
- No invalid links.
- No fake QR codes, payments, live chat or location.

## Content

- Unsigned projects marked DEMO.
- Unsigned partnerships marked discussion or demo.
- No unauthorized logos.
- No unauthorized images.
- No fake reviews.
- No fake ratings.
- No fake orders or users.
- No fake exclusivity.
- No fake discounts.

## Official Resources

- Emergency numbers checked.
- Online police entry checked.
- RATP lost property checked.
- Consular resources checked.
- Medical resources checked.
- External links checked.
- `verifiedAt` updated.

## Community

- Adults only.
- No public contact details.
- No precise address.
- No real-time location.
- No child profiles.
- Report and block flows.
- Community rules and safety plan.

## Legal

- Real entity.
- Mentions légales.
- Privacy.
- CGU.
- CGV if needed.
- Reviews rules.
- Rewards rules.
- Community rules.
- Cookie.
- Image rights.
- Contracts.
- Insurance.
- Guide and transport qualifications.
- Ticketing model.
- Tourism operator assessment.

## Travel Mart V5

- No real inventory or fake stock visible.
- No real product checkout enabled before CGV and consumer rules are ready.
- No fake delivery time or fake delivery partner.
- No hotel room number collection.
- Hotel front desk delivery policy checked.
- Food labels and allergens checked before any food product sale.
- Rental deposit, cleaning, damage, late return and return policies reviewed.
- Merchant contracts signed before partner claims.
- Image and logo authorization confirmed.
- Consumer complaint handling process ready.
- Data minimization checked.
- No alcohol, medicine, tobacco, e-cigarette, nicotine or high-risk regulated products listed.
