# Moments Marketplace Strategy

Moments covers travel photography, costume rentals, makeup/hair, proposals, anniversaries, birthdays, family photos and business photos.

MVP scope:

- Display demo services.
- Collect requests.
- Manual confirmation.
- No deposit.
- No rent.
- No schedule promise.
- No stock/size promise.

Partner categories:

- Photography studios.
- Costume rental studios.
- Makeup and hair artists.
- Legal transport providers when transport is involved.
- Restaurants or venues only after agreement.
