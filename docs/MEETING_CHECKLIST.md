# Meeting Checklist

## Museums

1. Accept external `guide-conférencier`?
2. Verify professional card?
3. Group threshold?
4. Max group size?
5. `Droit de parole` or `droit de visite`?
6. Audiophones required?
7. Lead time?
8. Chinese pilot timeslot possible?
9. Four-language page allowed?
10. Official image rights?
11. Logo use scope?
12. Text approval needed?
13. Group tickets?
14. Bulk tickets?
15. Family route possible?
16. Late/cancellation rules?
17. Closure handling?
18. Street plus museum route allowed?
19. Limited theme route?
20. 6-8 week pilot?

## Cruises

Ask about professional department, partner links, channel codes, e-tickets, group tickets, payment, refund, late arrival, weather, water level, pre-boarding Chinese assistance, live commentary approval, image rights and pilot timeslots.
