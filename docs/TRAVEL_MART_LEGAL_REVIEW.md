# Travel Mart Legal Review

Before direct sale of physical products, confirm:

- CGV and consumer information requirements.
- Price display, delivery terms and refund rules.
- Right of withdrawal rules.
- Food labeling, allergens and storage conditions.
- Consumer complaint process.
- Merchant contracts and liability split.
- Image, brand and logo authorization.
- Data minimization and privacy policy updates.

Forbidden in MVP:

- Medicine sales.
- Alcohol.
- Tobacco, e-cigarettes or nicotine products.
- High-risk regulated products.
- Fake merchant partnerships or fake stock.
