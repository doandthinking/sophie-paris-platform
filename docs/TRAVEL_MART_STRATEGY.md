# Travel Mart Strategy

V5 positions Paris Travel Mart as a request-intent and scenario-support layer, not a live e-commerce shop.

## MVP Scope

- Show demo travel essentials, rentals, service requests and official-resource redirects.
- Organize by traveler scenario: arrival, food comfort, devices, family, seniors, rain, photo memory and departure.
- Collect needs for manual confirmation.
- Use clear labels: DEMO, partner confirmation, stock pending, price pending, legal review required.

## Out Of Scope

- No real inventory.
- No warehouse.
- No real checkout.
- No fake stock.
- No fake delivery time.
- No high-risk regulated products.

## Next Validation

- Interview 3 Asian groceries or Chinese convenience stores.
- Interview 2 travel essentials stores.
- Test 10 traveler needs before enabling commerce.
